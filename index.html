BDO Hair Colour Lab

AI-assisted guidance for Black Desert Online hair colours.

BDO Hair Colour Lab helps players explore and recreate the hair colours they want for their characters, while accounting for the limitations of the game's custom colour system.

What it does

  • Explore hair colours from names, RGB values, or reference images.
  • Estimate colours that are suitable for BDO's custom colour picker.
  • Build coordinated Roots, Hair, and Tips colour recipes.
  • Explore alternative colours when the game adjusts a requested colour.

All colour recommendations are estimates. Results are not guaranteed to reproduce an exact in-game appearance.
