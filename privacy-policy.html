Privacy Policy

BDO Hair Colour Lab

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. About this policy

BDO Hair Colour Lab is an AI-assisted, skills-only plugin for ChatGPT. It helps Black Desert Online players explore hair colours and create colour recipes that take the game's custom-colour limitations into account.

The plugin consists of instructions and reference material that run within ChatGPT. It does not operate its own application server, database, analytics system, advertising service, or external API. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the plugin and when you contact its developer directly.

2. Information processed when you use the plugin

You may choose to include information in your ChatGPT conversation such as:

  • Text prompts and descriptions of the colour or hairstyle you want.
  • Colour names and RGB values.
  • Reference images, image links, and other content you choose to provide.
  • Related conversation content needed to answer your request.

This information is processed by ChatGPT to generate the plugin's responses. The plugin developer does not receive a separate copy of these prompts, images, or conversations through the plugin and does not independently store or maintain them. The developer cannot independently access your ChatGPT conversations through this plugin.

3. Platform processing

Because the plugin runs within ChatGPT, OpenAI processes conversation content under its own terms, privacy policy, service-provider arrangements, and applicable account or workspace settings. The plugin developer does not control OpenAI's processing, data controls, or retention practices.

For details, see OpenAI's Privacy Policy and OpenAI's Chat and File Retention guidance.

4. Support email

If you contact the developer at ericbdogpt@gmail.com, the developer will receive the sender's email address and any name, message content, attachments, or other information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your enquiry, troubleshoot an issue, and maintain the correspondence needed to resolve it.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Google processes that information as the email service provider under its own terms and privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing

The developer does not sell support correspondence or disclose it to unrelated third parties for advertising. Support information may be processed by the email provider and may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address security, abuse, or legal concerns. The plugin itself does not send user prompts or images to the developer or to a separate third-party service.

6. Retention

Plugin conversations and images: The developer does not keep an independent copy. OpenAI's retention rules and account controls apply to conversations and files submitted through ChatGPT. See OpenAI's Chat and File Retention guidance for current details.

Support email: The developer keeps support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, resolve the issue, maintain relevant correspondence, and meet applicable legal or security obligations. Email-provider storage and backup practices are governed by the provider's own policies.

7. Your choices

  • You decide what information and images to include in a ChatGPT conversation or support email.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information that is not needed for a hair-colour request.
  • You can manage ChatGPT conversations, files, and data controls using the controls provided by OpenAI.
  • You can request deletion of support correspondence by writing to the contact address below. Ongoing legal or security requirements may limit deletion.

For ChatGPT controls, see OpenAI's Data Controls guide.
